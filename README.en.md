# Evolved Three Kingdoms (進化三國)

[中文](README.md) | **English** | [日本語](README.ja.md)

Want to play Sanguosha but can't gather enough people? Here you get 90 generals, smart computer opponents, and online play with friends.

## Features
- **90 generals**: every skill is implemented; supports Identity mode, Shapeshift mode and ⚡ chain cards
- **Smart AI**: infers hidden identities and cooperates with teammates, without peeking at your hand
- **Play with friends**: create rooms, play online, spectate, and reconnect after a disconnect
- **Ranked ladder**: earn points in ranked games and unlock avatar frames, effects and achievements
- **Polished and handy**: 20 color themes, works on desktop, tablet and phone, with a beginner tutorial and a card gallery
- **No setup**: download, open, play

## Download
Get the latest version from **[Releases](https://github.com/jkemusic/Threekingdoms/releases)**:

- **Windows**: download the `.exe` installer or the portable `.zip`
- **Mac**: download the `.dmg` (on first launch, right-click → "Open")

## Card Art
Cards, generals and identity cards use ink-wash illustrations generated with SDXL, plus 11 card-face styles that each have their own frame. On the online server (<https://161.33.195.70.sslip.io/>) you can see them right in the browser.

This project does not bundle the original Sanguosha artwork (it is copyrighted; this project is non-profit and for friends only). To show your own images locally: menu "Game → Open assets folder", put them in `三國殺牌/` and `三國殺武將卡/`, then restart.

## Play with Friends
Menu "Game → LAN mode". Friends on the same network open `http://<your IP>:8765/` in a browser to join.
